About Carers Support

Supporting an invisible army.

Carers Support works across Bristol and South Gloucestershire with carers of all ages — including young carers who take on responsibilities well beyond their years. As the charity's own Fundraising Manager puts it, carers are a kind of invisible army: holding up huge parts of the country's care infrastructure, often with very little support behind them.

It matters more than it might first appear. A striking share of young carers end up not in education, employment or training — a statistic that genuinely unsettled the team when they looked at it properly. Raising the charity's profile, and the money that funds its work, is not an abstract exercise.

"We're a great small team. We want to develop, we want to evolve, and we want to grow with the person that's coming on board."Mark Smyth, Fundraising Manager
